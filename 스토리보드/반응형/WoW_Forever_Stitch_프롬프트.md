- Epic, heroic, cinematic high fantasy — like an official AAA game launch page.

- Text: ivory `#E8E2D6` (main), `#BFB7AA` (sub)
- Image overlays: black gradients only.

- Do not space out Korean letters (write "다시 태어난 전설", not "다 시 태 어 난").
